# Wellspring Hardware & Companion Technical Specification

**Device**: Wellspring Neuro-Haptic Wristband  
**Lead Strategist**: Ayush Shah  

---

## 1. Physical Architecture & Industrial Design
- **Dimensions**: 38 mm × 18 mm × 8.5 mm (ultra-low profile).
- **Weight**: 19.5 grams (negligible wrist fatigue during all-day classroom wear).
- **Enclosure**: Anodized aerospace aluminum with soft-touch matte finish.
- **Strap**: Medical-grade, hypoallergenic, sweat-resistant fluoropolymer elastomer with breathable micro-perforations.
- **Water Resistance**: IP68 certified (dust-tight and water immersion up to 1.5 meters).

---

## 2. Haptic & Sensory Subsystem
- **Actuator**: Linear Resonant Actuator (LRA) engineered for silent, high-precision haptic pulses without audible buzzing.
- **Frequency Range**: 35 Hz to 75 Hz (calibrated for somatosensory vagal nerve stimulation).
- **Haptic Waveforms**: Gentle wave pulse, heartbeat sync, breathing expand/contract cadence.

---

## 3. Connectivity & Power
- **Wireless Protocol**: Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE 5.3) with instant background reconnection.
- **Battery**: 140 mAh rechargeable Lithium-Polymer cell.
- **Battery Life**: 7 continuous days under typical usage (3 × 15-minute daily sessions).
- **Charging**: Magnetic pogo-pin dock (0 to 100% in 45 minutes).
