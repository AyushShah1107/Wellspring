# Product Management & Wearable Tech Competencies Demonstrated

**Project**: Wellspring Neuro-Haptic Wearable & Companion Ecosystem  
**Author & Product Lead**: **Ayush Shah**  

---

### 1. Zero-to-One Product Vision & Problem Framing
- Transformed an ambiguous high-level prompt (*"Design a product that brings boredom back, for good"*) into an actionable, scientifically grounded hardware-software solution.
- Grounded the value proposition in prefrontal neuroscience: isolating the role of the **Default Mode Network (DMN)** in divergent synthesis.

### 2. Market Sizing & Beachhead Segmentation
- Dissected India's 10.1M educator ecosystem into a Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM) of **720,000 tech-savvy private school teachers** and an actionable Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM) of **108,000 early adopters**.
- Selected teachers as the beachhead using a structured scoring formula ($S \times EO \times CD \times A / CoC$).

### 3. Quantitative Prioritization (RICE Framework)
- Evaluated candidate product features using formal RICE scoring:
  - Timed Nudges (RICE: 6.33, P0 MVP)
  - Ephemeral Idea Journaling (RICE: 5.70, P1 MVP)
  - Biometric Reports (RICE: 2.80, Phase 1)
  - Community Pods (RICE: 2.40, Phase 2)

### 4. Hardware + Software Ecosystem Definition
- Authored complete hardware PRD specifications (LRA haptics, BLE 5.3, battery longevity, industrial casing) coupled with mobile companion user journeys.
- Formulated the North Star Metric: **Average Minutes of Silent Stillness Sessions Completed per User per Day**.

### 5. Multi-Stage Commercialization & Business Modeling
- Architected a 3-stage revenue strategy transitioning from hardware margins (Rs. 2,499 unit sale) to high-margin recurring SaaS (Wellspring Pro @ Rs. 299/mo) and institutional B2B school wellness contracts.
